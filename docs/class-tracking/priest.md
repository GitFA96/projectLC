# Priest

## Rotation priorities (TBC guides)

**Shadow:** keep the full DoT suite rolling — **Vampiric Touch**, **Shadow
Word: Pain**, **Vampiric Embrace** — Mind Blast on cooldown, Mind Flay filler.
Five shadow spells maintain **Shadow Weaving**; the shadow priest also carries
**Misery** (hit for the caster group). Shadow priests are a mana battery: VT/VE
uptime is a raid service, not just personal DPS.

**Holy / Discipline:** triage healing; Power Infusion (disc) is an assignment
— it belongs on a mage/warlock on burn windows. Inner Focus + big heals,
Shadowfiend for mana, Pain Suppression as the tank-save.

## What we track

| Metric | Kind | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Vampiric Touch | debuff | Mana battery upkeep — the raid feels every gap |
| Shadow Word: Pain | debuff | Core DoT discipline |
| Vampiric Embrace | debuff | The healing-return service |
| Misery | debuff | The hit debuff the casters depend on |
| Shadow Weaving | debuff | The 5-stack shadow-damage amplifier |
| Power Infusion, Inner Focus, Shadowfiend, Pain Suppression | cooldowns | PI especially — an unused PI is a wasted raid cooldown |

## What we deliberately show little of

- **Inner Fire** — prep-level self-armor, ~100% uptime, ≥92% filter territory.
- **Renew / PW:S churn (holy/disc)** — healing throughput belongs to the HPS
  parse; shield/renew counts reward spam. (They're also filtered from the fight
  graph's damage view as incoming heals.)
- **Mind Flay clipping** — not measurable from aura events; parse territory.
