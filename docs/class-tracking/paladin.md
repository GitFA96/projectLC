# Paladin

## Rotation priorities (TBC guides)

**Holy:** Flash of Light / Holy Light triage, keep the assigned **Judgement**
(usually Wisdom or Light) on the boss, Divine Favor + Illumination for mana,
Lay on Hands as the emergency button.

**Protection (tank):** Righteous Fury on, keep **Holy Shield** rolling (the
block wall — uncrushability depends on it), Consecration + judgement in the
threat rotation, the assigned Judgement debuff maintained.

**Retribution:** Seal of Blood/Command twisting on swing timers, judgement on
cooldown, Crusader Strike on cooldown, Avenging Wrath on burn windows.

## What we track

| Metric | Kind | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Judgement of Wisdom / Light / the Crusader | debuff | Raid judgement assignments — mana return, heal return and crusader upkeep are jobs |
| Holy Shield | selfbuff | Prot's uncrushability upkeep — dropping it is a tank-death risk |
| Avenging Wrath, Divine Favor, Divine Illumination, Lay on Hands | cooldowns | Pressed or wasted |

## What we deliberately show little of

- **Seal uptime** — ret seal twisting *intentionally* churns seals every swing;
  "Seal of Blood uptime 40%" would read as a mistake when it's the technique.
  Twisting quality belongs to the parse.
- **Consecration casts** — AoE filler; counting rewards spam on single-target.
- **Blessings** (Might/Kings/Salvation…) — prep-level, ~100% uptime, covered by
  the raid-buff layer and filtered by the ≥92% rule.
- **Righteous Fury / auras** — on-or-off toggles, not upkeep.
