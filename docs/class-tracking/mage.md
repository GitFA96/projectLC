# Mage

## Rotation priorities (TBC guides)

**Fire (the raid spec):** the assigned mage keeps **5× Improved Scorch (Fire
Vulnerability)** stacked and refreshed; everyone else spams Fireball.
Combustion on cooldown/burn windows.

**Frost:** Frostbolt spam; one deep-frost mage maintains **Winter's Chill**
stacks for the frost group. Icy Veins + Cold Snap on cooldown.

**Arcane:** Arcane Blast stack management against mana; Arcane Power +
Presence of Mind on burn windows; Evocation on the mana plan.

## What we track

| Metric | Kind | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Imp. Scorch (Fire Vulnerability) | debuff | The fire-mage assignment — 5 stacks maintained is a job, and gaps hit every fire caster |
| Winter's Chill | debuff | The frost-group equivalent |
| Combustion, Arcane Power, Icy Veins, Presence of Mind, Cold Snap, Evocation | cooldowns | Pressed or wasted; Evocation counts show mana planning |

## What we deliberately show little of

- **Ignite** — the classic trap metric: it's a passive crit rollover. High
  Ignite uptime measures crit luck, not play. Fight-graph buff windows only.
- **Fireball/Frostbolt cast counts** — the parse prices throughput.
- **Arcane Blast stack uptime** — deliberately churned against mana; "uptime"
  would misread the technique (same reasoning as ret seal twisting).
- **Mage Armor / Molten Armor** — prep-level, ≥92% filter territory.
