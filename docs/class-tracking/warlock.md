# Warlock

## Rotation priorities (TBC guides)

**Everyone's first job: the curse assignment.** One warlock carries **Curse of
the Elements** (or Shadow pre-2.4 folding), one **Curse of Recklessness** /
**Curse of Tongues** where assigned, the rest run **Curse of Doom/Agony** for
personal damage. A dropped assignment curse hits every caster in the raid.

**Destruction (shadow):** Shadow Bolt spam + the curse; (fire builds keep
**Immolate** up).

**Affliction:** keep the full DoT suite rolling — **Unstable Affliction**,
**Corruption**, Siphon Life — plus the curse, Shadow Bolt filler, Life Tap to
feed it.

## What we track

| Metric | Kind | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Curse of the Elements / Recklessness / Shadow / Tongues | debuff | Raid assignments — uptime by boss shows exactly who dropped which curse when |
| Curse of Doom / Agony | debuff | The personal-damage curses (also shows nobody wasted an assignment slot) |
| Corruption | debuff | Core DoT discipline, both specs |
| Unstable Affliction | debuff | Affliction's signature upkeep |
| Immolate | debuff | Fire-destro upkeep |

(No warlock cooldowns tracked — TBC warlocks have no major throughput CD;
Death Coil/Shadowfury are situational, not graded.)

## What we deliberately show little of

- **Life Tap** — mana churn; counting it rewards nothing.
- **Siphon Life** — low-impact affliction filler; left out to keep the lanes
  readable. Add it if a deep-affliction lock joins the core.
- **Shadow Trance / proc windows** — luck; fight-graph buff windows only.
- **Pet management** (Blood Pact, imp phase) — prep-level or passive.
